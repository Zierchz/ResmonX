Battery section.

## Changes
- **Battery section**: new sidebar tab with charge level, state (charging,
  discharging, fully charged or charge limited), power draw in watts with a
  sparkline, estimated time remaining, battery health (full charge vs design
  capacity), capacity, voltage and Windows battery saver. The battery card
  also shows up in the Overview summary. On desktops without a battery the
  section shows a notice and everything else keeps working.

## Install
Download and run `ResmonX-Setup.exe` (or `resmonx_0.8.0_x64-setup.exe`).
Requires Windows 10/11.
